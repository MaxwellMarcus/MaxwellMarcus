# Maxwell Marcus

**Princeton Mathematics ’28 · Quantum information theory · Quantum software**

I am an undergraduate at Princeton working at the intersection of mathematical
quantum information and research software. My current work studies the
Clifford hierarchy, generalized semi-Clifford structure, and the finite groups
generated by iterated Pauli conjugation.

I am especially interested in quantum algorithms, error correction and fault
tolerance, circuit synthesis, compilers, and software that turns mathematical
structure into testable computational claims.

## Current research

I am leading a primarily self-directed project advised by Marcel Dall’Agnol
(Princeton) and Sathya Subramanian (Oxford). We prove generalized
semi-Clifford structure for third- and fourth-level Clifford-hierarchy gates in
prime-dimensional qudit systems and introduce higher-order conjugation groups
as a tool for studying the hierarchy.

Our manuscript, *The Second-Conjugation Clifford Group of a Fourth-Level Qudit
Gate*, is currently under review at ITCS 2027. A public preprint link will be
added when it becomes available.

## Featured projects

### [Generalized Semi-Clifford Tester](https://github.com/MaxwellMarcus/generalized-semi-clifford-tester)

`Python` · `NumPy` · `Qiskit` · `pytest` · `GitHub Actions`

A research-oriented toolkit for testing generalized semi-Clifford structure on
small qubit systems.

- Implements a definition-first exhaustive tester over binary Lagrangian
  subspaces and returns independently checkable witnesses.
- Preserves `UNKNOWN` outcomes when numerical evidence or search limits do not
  justify a mathematical conclusion.
- Includes experimental Qiskit circuits for Pauli-conjugation sampling,
  semi-Clifford testing, and GSC candidate verification with finite-shot
  confidence bounds.

### [Clifford Hierarchy Conjugation Testing](https://github.com/MaxwellMarcus/clifford-hierarchy-conjugation-testing)

`Python` · `NumPy` · `SymPy` · `finite-field methods` · `pytest`

Exact and numerical tools for studying conjugation groups in the Clifford
hierarchy.

- Provides validated abstractions for quantum gates, projective gate sets,
  bounded group closure, and iterated conjugation groups.
- Separates exact symbolic verification from tolerance-dependent numerical
  evidence and explicitly records incomplete searches.
- Reproduces and independently verifies a five-qubit counterexample from
  current Clifford-hierarchy research.
